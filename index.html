I

A century beneath the shadow

The world after the first betrayal

✧

A hundred years ago, a priest succumbed to temptation and opened the Red Gate.

Through a wound torn between worlds came the legions of Hell. Kingdoms fell. Fields became graves. And when the heavens answered, they placed their light in mortal hands.

Now paladins, priests, and saints stand between the Abyss and everything it has not yet taken. This is an age of miracles and mourning. An age in which hope is an act of defiance.

II

The last light behind the walls

Yoria

✧

Beyond the smoke, gardens still bloom. Golden wheat bends beneath the wind. Cathedral bells cross streets carved in stone, and people gather beneath the spires as though tomorrow is something they can promise one another.

To lose hope would be to concede the world. The kingdom of Yoria has not conceded.

A crown too heavy for the young

The king who must endure

Twenty years old, and already carrying the weight of a dying world. Levis IV rules with intelligence, compassion, and an increasingly painful understanding of what survival demands.

III

The divine flame

The light and its price

✧

The Saint

A vessel of miracles: restoration, cleansing, and wards that can repel even Diablo. Saints bear divine power without burning away the humanity and life-force others spend.

The Paladin

A sworn protector, trained to stand where prayers meet steel. Holy warriors spend life-force through their divine gifts

The Priest

A keeper of blessings, healing, and sacred rites. Some covenants surrender sight or voice to deepen their faith. Their power can sustain those who hold the line.

A hand held. A quiet embrace. A night of rest.
What battle consumes, care may help rekindle. Not every promise must be romantic.

IV

The Rite of Binding

Every oath waits for a voice

✧

Before the altar, the willing present themselves. The Saint may choose one, many, or none. Neither rank nor standing erases that freedom.

An oath waits behind every seal. Lift the veil to discover those who might walk beside you.

Covenant of the Unseeing

Zion

Faith beyond sight

Shunned in childhood, Zion found purpose through faith and the Covenant of the Unseeing. Beneath his white blindfold, his eyes are gone; a glimpse of the future guides his blessed longbow. Quiet and slow to trust, he offers kindness without spectacle. His affection is gentle, hesitant, and profoundly devoted to the one who lets him stay.

Sisters of Mercy

Three sisters. One terrible mercy.

Agnes, the unmasked eldest, hunts with a heavy crossbow and a cigar. Masked Velka reloads with ritual precision. Rita, the youngest, kneels to pray—not for themselves, but for the demons they fight. Together they give mercy a meaning that makes even Hell’s smiles falter.

VIII

The betrayal

The wound in the world

✧

A hunger that opened Hell

The Red Gate

A priest was promised wealth, dominion, and adoration. The rite he completed tore open the veil—and a century of suffering followed.

More than sixty metres of stone frame the passage to Hell. Souls wail through its red light. A Sin Sovereign guards the threshold while thousands of lesser demons prepare to march.

Closing the Gate, or killing its master, offers humanity its chance at salvation.

✧

The Sin Sovereigns

✧Sin SovereignPride

Sin Sovereign

Pride

False divinity

Radiant and angel-like, with obsidian wings and burning blue eyes, Pride wears the shape of salvation. His presence forces mortals toward worship until devotion becomes madness. He offers the comfort of a god who answers—and the horror of discovering what that answer demands. “I am what your gods failed to be.”

✧Sin SovereignWrath

Sin Sovereign

Wrath

Violence without direction

A gigantic warrior who thrives in slaughter, Wrath prefers a greataxe in each hand and welcomes every challenge. His influence turns battlefields into uncontrollable frenzy, stripping violence of purpose until only more violence remains. He does not seek an ending to war. “There is no victory. Only more war.”

✧Sin SovereignEnvy

Sin Sovereign

Envy

The face you thought you knew

Faceless beneath its borrowed shapes, Envy steals the appearances of mortals and carries only their short-term memories. Familiarity is its weapon; physical contact can cost a victim their face or soul. The portrait before you is no promise of a true form. “Don’t worry… I’ll take good care of your life.”
